## Why this email is the standard (Kanu's own words → these drive the rubric)

- **Relevance / match (load-bearing #1):** the email ties directly to the actual JD ("Applied AI
  work") AND to the work he's currently doing. The pivot line says *this role is the closer match
  for what I do* — it's specific to the req, not generic interest.
- **Urgency via social proof (load-bearing #2):** paragraph two names real, currently-live competing
  processes (two Walmart internal roles incl. "interview Friday", BCG X Senior AI). This induces
  #fomo and puts him on a visible timeline, so the recruiter feels a reason to move faster — without
  making Google Cloud the backup (the pivot keeps enthusiasm on *them*).
- **The thing most AI drafts get wrong that this gets right:** the urgency is REAL and dated, woven
  into one clean sentence with the why-this-company pivot — not bolted on, not invented, not padded.
- **If he could keep one trait:** the second paragraph — the live-process social-proof + "closer
  match" pivot working together.
