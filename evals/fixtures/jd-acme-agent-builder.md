# Acme — Senior Agent Builder

## About the role

Acme is building the platform that lets every team ship production AI agents
without a research budget. We are looking for a Senior Agent Builder to own
the end-to-end design, build, and operation of LLM-powered agents that solve
real customer problems at scale.

You will work cross-functionally with product, engineering, and design to
turn ambiguous problems into reliable agent systems. This is a hands-on
builder role — you prototype, ship, and iterate.

## What you will do

- Design and ship production LLM agents: prompt scaffolds, tool-use
  orchestration, eval harnesses, and guardrails.
- Own the agent lifecycle from prototype to production: build, evaluate,
  deploy, monitor, and iterate.
- Build retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines grounded in customer
  data; own the chunking, embedding, and retrieval strategy.
- Write and maintain evaluation suites that catch regressions before
  customers do; treat evals as a first-class deliverable.
- Collaborate with platform engineering to integrate agents into the
  broader product surface.

## What we are looking for

- 5+ years building and shipping production software systems.
- Demonstrated experience operating LLM agents in production — not just
  prototypes, but systems with real users and real consequences.
- Strong software engineering fundamentals; you write tests, you review
  code, you care about operational excellence.
- Experience with RAG, tool-use / function-calling, and prompt engineering
  at a level beyond calling the chat completions API.
- Comfort with ambiguity: you can take a fuzzy problem, decompose it, and
  ship a first version in days, not quarters.

## Nice to have

- Experience with MCP (Model Context Protocol) or similar agent
  interoperability standards.
- Background in experimentation and measurement — you think in hypotheses
  and confidence intervals.
- Prior experience with regulated industrial systems — Acme's agents
  run inside a tokamak-grade compute environment and we need someone who
  understands plasma containment as well as prompt design.

## Location

Remote, United States.

## Compensation

$180k–$240k base + equity.
