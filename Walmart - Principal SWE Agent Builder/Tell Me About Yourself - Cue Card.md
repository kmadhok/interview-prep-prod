## Full version (for practice)

Today I'm a Senior Data Analyst at Walmart Data Ventures supporting Customer Perception — I'm the point of contact for about 30 people across business and technical teams who come to me with analytics, stewardship, and readiness questions. Pretty quickly I realized the volume of that intake wasn't something one person could keep up with, so I started building — both probabilistic and deterministic systems — to absorb it. That's grown into Power BI dashboards, Python pipelines, a Jira resolution agent that stakeholders interacted with for months without realizing it wasn't a person, an autonomous data analyst, and a self-growing semantic layer that refines its own definitions over time. Earlier this year that work earned me a Making a Difference Award for what I shipped in 2025. What pulls me toward Agent Builder is that I've been doing this scoped to one team — the role makes it possible to do it at the scale Walmart actually operates at, and that's the version of this work I want to be doing.

## How to use this

- Practice with the full version until the **beat order** is automatic

- If you forget your place, the 5 section headers (Present → Shift → Built → Recognition → Why) are your recovery map
- End on the closing line cleanly, then stop talking — silence is the cue for them to ask their follow-up
