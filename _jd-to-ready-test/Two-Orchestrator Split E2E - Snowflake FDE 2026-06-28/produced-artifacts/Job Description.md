## About the Role

Snowflake is "powering the era of the agentic enterprise" and seeks AI-native thinkers who treat AI as a high-trust collaborator. Forward Deployed Analytics Engineers combine domain expertise with full-stack data and analytics engineering. You embed directly with customer data, analytics, and business teams to build the data foundations that power Snowflake's AI platform.

Focused on the layers that make AI reliable: clean, well-modeled data, governed pipelines, and semantic models that expose business meaning to natural language interfaces. You design rigorous data models, build and instrument pipelines, and construct the semantic layer between raw data and AI agents. When you leave an engagement, the customer's data is structured, trusted, and agent-ready. Deployment patterns and product gaps you surface feed back to Cortex product and research teams.

## Main Responsibilities

**Data Modeling and Architecture**
- Architect flexible, performant data models that drive customers toward single sources of truth.
- Use SQL, Python, dbt, and Snowflake to build and maintain data infrastructure for reporting, analysis, and automation.
- Perform data QA and develop automated testing procedures for Snowflake data models.
- Provide input into data governance strategies including permissions, data lineage, and data definitions.

**Semantic Layer and Agent Readiness**
- Build semantic data models that expose customer tables to natural language queries via Cortex Analyst.
- Define and validate the metrics, dimensions, and relationships that AI agents need to reason correctly.
- Identify and resolve gaps in data structure, naming, and coverage that would cause an agent to fail.

**Enablement and Knowledge Transfer**
- Build documented playbooks, reusable data model templates, and semantic model libraries customers can maintain.
- Run technical workshops to upskill customer data and analytics teams.
- Author semantic view configurations and skill files (YAML + Markdown) a non-technical analyst can invoke in plain English.

- Daily use of an AI coding assistant as a primary dev tool.
- Proficient in SQL (window functions, complex joins without docs).
- Experience with dbt or equivalent data modeling framework.
- Shipped at least one production data model or pipeline that non-technical business users relied on.
- Comfortable in Git (PRs, branches, code review).
- Demonstrable experience translating business requirements into technical specifications.

## Why Join

Most analytics engineering stops at the data model; most field roles stop at the recommendation. This role owns the full data stack from source ingestion to semantic layer, ensuring every layer is clean, tested, and structured for AI agents. You go onsite, write the code, build the semantic foundation, and stay until it runs in production and the customer team can maintain it.
