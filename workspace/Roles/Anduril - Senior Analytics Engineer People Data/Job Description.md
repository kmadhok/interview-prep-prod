## About the Job

Central to the People Data & Analytics team — build and maintain the data infrastructure that powers strategic HR insights. Own the full data lifecycle: ingest/integrate diverse HR data sources, design/develop/optimize data models and pipelines, transform raw disparate information into clean, reliable, analytics-ready datasets that empower People Analysts and business stakeholders.

## What You'll Do

- Design, build, optimize robust ETL/ELT pipelines to ingest/integrate/transform people data from HR systems (HRIS, ATS, LMS, etc.) into the data platform.
- Develop, maintain, govern scalable/secure data models, schemas, and ontologies for people analytics; ensure data quality, consistency, accessibility.
- Contribute to the strategic design and evolution of the people data platform and tooling (e.g., SQLMesh, Iceberg, Flyte); advocate engineering best practices and automation.
- Partner with People Analysts, HRBPs, and stakeholders to translate analytical needs into robust data solutions; deliver well-structured, documented, reliable datasets.
- Implement/monitor data quality checks; troubleshoot data issues; ensure reliability and integrity.
- Monitor pipeline/model performance, fix bottlenecks, ensure efficiency and scalability.
- Maintain documentation; champion data-engineering best practices (version control, testing, CI/CD).
- Enforce data security; ensure compliance with internal policy and external regs (GDPR, CCPA) on employee-data privacy.
- Collaborate with broader enterprise analytics/data-engineering teams on data architecture standards.

## Required Qualifications

- 5+ years in Data Engineering / Analytics Engineering building and optimizing pipelines and infrastructure.
- Expert SQL (complex manipulation/querying) + advanced Python (scripting, data processing, automation).
- Extensive cloud DW experience (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Databricks/Delta Lake) and data lakes (S3, ADLS).
- Robust data modeling (dimensional modeling, Kimball methodology) for analytics.
- Hands-on ETL/ELT with modern tools (dbt, Airflow, Flyte, Dagster, or similar).
- Excellent communication; translate technical concepts for non-technical stakeholders.
- Bachelor's in CS/Engineering/Data Science/IS or related.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Big-data frameworks (Spark, Flink); schema evolution / time travel (Iceberg, Delta Lake).
- Stack tools: Palantir Foundry, SQLMesh, Flyte.
- Cloud certs (AWS Data Analytics, GCP Professional Data Engineer).
- IaC/containers (Terraform, CloudFormation, Docker, Kubernetes).
- BI integration (Tableau, Power BI, Looker).
- HRIS systems (Rippling, Workday, Oracle HCM Cloud) data models/APIs.
- HR data concepts/metrics; interest in People Analytics.
