## About Artos

At Artos, we build tools that help biopharma companies create and manage their R&D documentation in a fraction of the time. Life sciences companies spend $13 billion and millions of man-hours each year on regulatory submissions for health agencies; Artos builds AI and software tools to shrink those timelines so life-saving treatments get to patients sooner. Artos helps clinical development and medical writing teams turn their data into regulatory submissions in days instead of weeks. (Biotechnology Research; 2–10 employees.)

## About the Role

We're growing fast and looking for an engineer who thrives in a high-velocity environment and wants to do meaningful work. As a core member of Artos's engineering team, you'll play a critical role in developing, scaling, and expanding the Artos platform to serve regulatory needs for pharma and life science companies around the globe.
