## About

CZI was founded in 2015 by Priscilla Chan and Mark Zuckerberg to harness technology for ambitious goals — from curing/preventing disease to transforming how students learn. Flagship nonprofits: Biohub and Learning Commons. Working at the intersection of AI and biology.

## The Team — Investments

The Investments team builds a world-class investment portfolio to fund CZI's ongoing operations long term, pursuing a "manager of managers" approach — finding extraordinary investment organizations across styles and asset classes.

## The Opportunity

As an Applied AI Engineer on the Investments team, you will help build AI-powered applications and workflows that enhance investment research, decision-making, and operational efficiency. Working closely with investment and operations professionals, you will develop tools and data solutions that make information more accessible, automate manual processes, and generate actionable insights across the investment lifecycle.

**This is an opportunity for a new grad or early-career engineer** excited about applying AI, software engineering, and data technologies to real-world challenges. You'll gain exposure to financial markets, institutional investing, and modern AI systems.

## What You'll Do

- Build AI-enabled applications and workflows that support investment research, manager monitoring, reporting, and operational processes.
- Develop integrations with internal and external data sources to improve access to information and reduce manual work.
- Contribute to data pipelines, automation workflows, and reporting systems that improve efficiency and data accessibility.
- Organize, process, and prepare structured and unstructured data for use in AI applications and analytics tools.
- Implement data validation, monitoring, and quality checks to improve the reliability of investment data.
- Partner with investment and operations team members to understand business needs and develop technical solutions.
- Explore and prototype emerging AI technologies, including large language models, retrieval systems, and workflow automation tools.
- Participate in code reviews, testing, documentation, and software engineering best practices.

## What You'll Bring

- Bachelor's or Master's in CS, SWE, Math, Stats, Engineering, Finance, Economics, or related, or equivalent practical experience.
- Strong interest in financial markets, investing, and applying technology to improve investment decision-making.
- Strong programming skills in Python or similar; experience building software through internships, research, coursework, hackathons, or personal projects.
- Solid CS fundamentals: algorithms, data structures, software design, APIs, databases, data processing.
- Interest in AI, LLMs, workflow automation, and emerging technologies.
- Exposure to SWE, ML, data science, or quantitative research.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, written communication, and collaboration skills.
- Familiarity with modern dev tools, cloud, data analysis, or investment concepts is a plus.
