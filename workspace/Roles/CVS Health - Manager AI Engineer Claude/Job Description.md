## Job summary

CVS Health is hiring a **Manager, AI Engineer** to serve as a **hands-on technical contributor** driving adoption and expansion of the **Anthropic Claude platform** across the enterprise. You will design and build AI-powered integrations, workflows, and tooling that connect Claude to the systems and data teams rely on. A technical role for an engineer who wants to work at the forefront of enterprise AI.

## What you will do

- Design, build, and maintain integrations between the **Anthropic Claude platform and enterprise systems** using the **Claude API and MCP servers**.
- Develop and manage **MCP server configurations** to extend Claude's capabilities and connect it to internal tools, databases, and third-party services.
- Build and optimize **prompt pipelines, agentic workflows, and automations** that solve real business problems at scale.
- Evaluate and implement integration patterns for connecting Claude to enterprise platforms such as **Slack, Microsoft 365, Jira, and internal data sources**.
- Troubleshoot complex technical issues across the AI stack, from API behavior to MCP tool execution.
- Write clean, well-documented code and contribute to shared standards for building on top of Claude.
- Test and iterate on AI workflows to improve reliability, performance, and output quality.
- Stay current on Anthropic platform updates, new model capabilities, and the evolving MCP ecosystem.

## Required qualifications

- 5+ years in software engineering or a related technical discipline.
- 2+ years using Python and/or JavaScript, with hands-on experience building and consuming APIs.
- 1–2 years with **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**, including building or configuring MCP servers and tools.
- 1–3 years with **agentic AI patterns, prompt engineering, and LLMs in production**.
- 1–3 years with the **Anthropic Claude platform** inclusive of Claude Code and Claude Chat.
- Must be able to work East Coast business hours.

## Preferred qualifications

- Ability to communicate technical concepts clearly to non-technical stakeholders.
- Experience in healthcare or a highly regulated industry.
- Familiarity with enterprise security and governance considerations around AI.
- Strong debugging and problem-solving across distributed systems and API-driven architecture.
- Experience integrating AI or SaaS platforms with enterprise systems and internal tooling.
