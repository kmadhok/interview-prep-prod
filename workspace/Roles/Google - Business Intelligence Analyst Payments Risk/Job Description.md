## About the Job

Risk Frameworks and Standards owns Alphabet's Enterprise Risk Management program — defining/maintaining the ERM framework and top-line key risk indicators, standardized policy artifacts, policy oversight, training, and business risk assessments. **Payments Risk Management** focuses on payments-specific enterprise risks on the front line of regulatory needs: developing/enhancing payments risk frameworks, policies, assessments, and reporting, working cross-functionally with internal and external teams.

## Responsibilities

- Collect, analyze, and interpret large datasets related to risk activities.
- Develop and maintain key metrics; suggest thresholds, dashboards, and reports to visualize metrics across slices of data.
- Ensure accuracy, completeness, and integrity of risk data; collaborate with engineering partners and other stakeholders to improve data management and validation processes.
- Present data insights to technical and non-technical audiences; contribute to improving risk and compliance processes.
- Leverage AI models to enhance capabilities, including for risk assessments and monitoring programs.

## Minimum Qualifications

- 4 years of experience in business intelligence or data analytics.
- Experience with data analysis tools and programming languages (e.g., SQL, R, Google Sheets).

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience in/with enterprise risk management.
- Experience with statistical modeling and machine learning.
- Strong AI-forward thinking and experience with Prompt Engineering.
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
