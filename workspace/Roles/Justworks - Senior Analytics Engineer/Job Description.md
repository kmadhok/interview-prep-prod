## About the team

The Data & Analytics team is Justworks' data and AI center of excellence — a connected set of functions spanning business intelligence, data science, analytics engineering, platform data/ML engineering and AI builders. Grown significantly over the last several years; leveling up maturity in how they deliver insights, govern data, and enable the company to make better decisions.

## Who you are

A data practitioner with a strong track record for designing, building, and deploying models within a medallion architecture to accelerate reporting, insights, and self-service capabilities. Strong understanding of how to use data to answer business questions; passionate about unlocking that capability for data teams and business stakeholders. Enjoys partnering closely with stakeholders, translating their questions into trustworthy data products. Keen sense of the big picture; can drive projects toward realistic, rewarding business outcomes.

As a Senior Analytics Engineer, you will be a key contributor in helping the organization derive more value from its datasets through scalable, modular design of enterprise data models and the semantic layer. You will work closely with data engineers, analysts, and business stakeholders to develop & iterate on data requirements, put data models into production, and surface them through governed BI.

## What you will work on

- Transform raw data into high-quality data models easily queried and understood by various business units (Sales, Marketing, Product, Engineering, Operations)
- Design, build, and maintain the semantic layer using Snowflake semantic views — defining metrics, entities, and business logic so stakeholders can self-serve trustworthy, consistently-defined data without re-deriving logic
- Partner with analysts and business teams to build and govern BI content in Tableau and Redash — curated data sources, certified dashboards, reusable metrics — and reduce dashboard sprawl through consolidation and clear standards
- Partner directly with stakeholders to translate business questions into data requirements, co-develop metric definitions, and drive adoption of governed models and dashboards
- Proactively identify opportunities to close gaps in reporting and insights generation by architecting new business facts and dimensions
- Apply effective data testing and sensitive data management strategies to ensure quality and security of information
- Level up information design, data governance, and analytics engineering practices — define internal standards for lineage, maintainability, performance, and cost management
- Provide technical leadership and mentorship to junior and mid-level team members — best practices in data modeling, orchestration, data warehouse architecture, data privacy, and analytics enablement
