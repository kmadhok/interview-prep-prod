## About the Team

The Accelerated Pickup & Delivery (APD) program is Walmart's in-store fulfillment automation initiative, deploying automated systems across stores with a vision to scale to 400+ sites, unlocking a $20B+ P&L impact. The APD Automation Engineering & Strategy team sits at the intersection of fulfillment strategy, Technology, and Operations — owning the performance narrative for one of Walmart's most ambitious automation investments. The team drives cross-site performance benchmarking, long-range investment analysis, automated fulfillment strategy, and — critically — the AI-powered storytelling and visualization capabilities that translate complex automation data into executive-ready insights/decisions.

## About the Role

As Senior Manager, Automation Engineering, you will own the end-to-end development and delivery of APD's performance reporting, strategic storytelling, and AI-powered communication capabilities. You are equal parts engineer, strategist, and storyteller. You build strategies and use visual and AI tools to turn raw automation data into compelling narratives that drive executive decisions. You will be the person who "connects the dots" — breaking down complex priorities into smart, brief, leadership-ready communications. You'll build APD's reporting frameworks, write white papers on the automation and fulfillment strategy, design best-in-class visualizations, and use AI (agents, prompting, video, content generation) to accelerate the team's communication.
