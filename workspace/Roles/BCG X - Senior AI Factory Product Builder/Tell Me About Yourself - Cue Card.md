## The 4 beats (glance-only triggers)

1. **HOOK — the bottleneck.** "Data analyst on Customer Perception (1M-member panel) → I'm the bottleneck between the data and 30+ business & technical folks."
2. **PROOF — the agent.** "400+ Jira tickets since Jan · 30 min–full day each → under 10 min, async, 20 at once."
3. **EVOLUTION + ADOPTION.** "Built on a context layer → evolved into a deterministic semantic layer. Today my team runs these as skills/agents in their own coding CLIs."
4. **WHY THIS ROLE.** "That's the AI Factory role — build AI assets teams across a *firm* adopt, not just my 30."

## Full script (your words, tightened — internalize, don't memorize)

> "I'm the data analyst on Customer Perception — a proprietary million-member research panel inside Walmart Data Ventures — which makes me the bottleneck between the data and the 30-plus business and technical folks on the team. Every data analysis, readiness, and stewardship question routes through me.
>
> Since January that's been 400-plus Jira tickets, each anywhere from 30 minutes to a full day depending on complexity. So I built the **Jira Resolution Agent** — it now takes under 10 minutes, runs asynchronously, so I can fire it at 20 tickets at once.
>
> It's built on a **context layer** — getting the model the right context at the right time to understand the problem — which I've since evolved into a **deterministic semantic layer** so the definitions are governed, not just generated. And today it's not just mine: my team runs these as **skills and agents inside their own coding CLIs**.
>
> That last part is what pulls me to the AI Factory role — doing exactly this, but building AI assets that teams across an entire *firm* adopt, not just my 30."

## TRAPS — watch these

- ❌ **Savings range in the opener.** Never "17–40 workdays saved." The 30-min-to-a-day *input* range is fine (it justifies impact); just don't reintroduce a savings range. Aggregate only if asked.
- ❌ **Close ends on Walmart.** Your instinct is to end on "enable my teams." Push past it to **BCG + firm scale** every time — that's the *why this role* answer.
- ❌ **Buried hook.** Don't spend 2 sentences explaining the panel before "I'm the bottleneck." Panel = one clause, then bottleneck.
- ❌ **Slogan tic.** "Right [X] at the right time" — once, not three times.
- ⚠️ **Whisper note:** it transcribes "Jira"→"geo", "Resolution"→"Solution", "Data Ventures"→"DataVengers". Not your error — but verify the transcript if you're reading it back, and in the room say *JEER-uh Resolution Agent* clearly.
