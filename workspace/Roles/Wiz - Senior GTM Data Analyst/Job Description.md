## Summary

The Go-To-Market (GTM) Analytics team enables data-driven decision-making across Wiz. As Wiz scales as part of Google Cloud, the need for actionable insights, reliable data foundations, and data-driven strategies has never been greater.

Seeking a highly motivated, **AI-forward** individual. Reporting to the Senior Manager, GTM Analytics, the Senior GTM Data Analyst acts as a dedicated analytics business partner — owning core models, dashboards, ad hoc analyses, and insights that drive execution and strategic priorities. Work closely with senior and executive stakeholders. The strongest candidates have strong analytical instincts, curiosity, judgment, and initiative to learn quickly, experiment with new tools, and **create leverage beyond the boundaries of a traditional analytics role**.

## What You'll Do

- Serve as a primary analytics partner to GTM leadership — shape business questions, define requirements, deliver insights that influence strategic and operational decisions.
- Build, maintain, and improve dashboards, self-service reporting, and analytical tools for fast, accurate visibility into performance, trends, opportunities.
- Own and improve core data models and pipelines, using tools such as **dbt**, to develop well-structured, reliable, scalable datasets powering GTM analytics.
- Partner with IT, BI, Data, Operations, Finance, and Business teams to strengthen data infrastructure, improve data quality, align on standardized metrics/definitions/methodologies.
- Conduct advanced analytical work: exploratory deep dives, statistical analysis, regressions, segmentation, funnel analysis.
- **Use AI and modern analytical tools to increase the speed, quality, and scope of your work** — analysis, documentation, prototyping, automation, data exploration.
- Represent Analytics in cross-functional initiatives — identify opportunities to improve workflows, automate repetitive work, build scalable solutions that create leverage for the broader team.

## What You'll Bring

- **3–4+ years** in analytics, data operations, consulting, business intelligence, revenue operations, or similar.
- Strong analytical fundamentals: advanced SQL, complex datasets, data models, dashboards, reporting systems.
- Clear communication and business judgment — translate ambiguous business problems into actionable recommendations for senior stakeholders.
- Curiosity and technical adaptability — new tools, AI-enabled workflows, Python / modern analytical modeling.
- Familiarity with **VSCode, GitHub, BigQuery, dbt, Python, Looker** or similar is a plus; learning speed matters more than any specific tool.

## Who Thrives In This Role

Analytics as more than reporting: energized by understanding how the business works, identifying the highest-leverage problems, using whatever tools are available (including AI) to move faster, think deeper, produce better work. A strong analyst at the core who stretches into data engineering, automation, business strategy, workflow design, and tool-building. "The best analysts increasingly operate like **one-person teams**" — analytical rigor + technical fluency + business judgment + creativity for outsized impact.
