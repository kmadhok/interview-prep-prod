## About This Role

Join a diverse and collaborative team of over 300 modelers and technologists in Aladdin Financial Engineering (AFE) within BlackRock Solutions, the business responsible for the research and development of Aladdin's financial models. The models span equities, fixed income, commodities, derivatives, and private markets.

## Role Description

Join the **Advanced Data Analytics** team within AFE Single Security — a team of Quantitative Data Specialists focused on delivering Research & Derived Data, Governance and Research Platform. The team leverages data, cloud, and emerging technologies to deliver research and production data/data solutions for core analytics and investment teams in the Single Security space, using statistical/mathematical methodologies to derive insights and generate content for predictive models, clustering, and classification solutions and to enable Governance. Works on Mortgages, Structured Products, Credit Products, and Private Markets.

This person will work on the **Structured Product space** with a focus on public structured products, contributing as SME on structured products. Will leverage Big Data technologies and data platforms like **Snowflake, Databricks** to build out a comprehensive analytics offering backed by enhanced data, as well as **leverage AI for efficiency and agentic automation**. Will partner with Investment teams and clients to generate Investment Intelligence.
