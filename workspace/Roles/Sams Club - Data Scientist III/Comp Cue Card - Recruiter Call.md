**Call:** Recruiter reaches out Mon 6/15 to schedule the comp conversation. **This call is the comp negotiation.**
**Posture:** Happy in current role, not desperate. Real BATNA (BCG X round 2 Tue 6/16, Walmart Principal DA round Fri 6/19). Negotiate from strength.

## The one rule: make them name a number first

When asked "what are your expectations?", **don't anchor — redirect to the band:**

> "Since this is an internal move, I'd want to understand how DS III is scoped in the band and how it maps from my current grade before I put a number on it. What range are you working with?"

If they press for a number anyway, give the anchor below. Otherwise, **get the band + grade mapping first, then drop your number.**

## Three facts to extract on the call

1. **Grade mapping** — Is DS III a true level-up from Senior Data Analyst, or a lateral title change?
2. **Band placement** — Where in the band is *this* role scoped (entry / mid / senior end)?
3. **Location / RPZ** — JD says Bentonville only; you're Chicago-based. Remote/Chicago vs. relocate swings base 10%+ via Regional Pay Zone. Resolve before committing to any number.

Also capture: **bonus target %**, **equity refresh**, **req/job ID**, your **exact current base** (bring it — the internal bump is often pegged to it).

---

## What to hold back

- Don't volunteer that you're time-pressured. **Their** deadline (~6/19) is leverage on their side, not yours.
- Don't over-share the BCG / Principal DA timing as urgency. You *can* mention you're in other late-stage processes — it raises your value — but frame it as "evaluating options together," not "hurry."
- "Let me think about it" is a complete sentence. Costless for you, expensive for them.

## Bottom line

Don't anchor → make them bid → hold a private floor north of $155K → let BCG (6/16) and Principal DA (6/19) play out before committing. **Every outcome is fine.** That's your leverage.
