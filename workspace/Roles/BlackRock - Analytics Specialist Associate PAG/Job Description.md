## About the Portfolio Analytics Group (PAG)

A highly collaborative and multidisciplinary team of **in-house, forward-deployed engineers and data scientists** at the forefront of applied analytics at BlackRock. As part of the Aladdin Financial Engineering (AFE) division, PAG develops and deploys advanced analytical solutions that integrate Aladdin's technology platform, financial models, and data to solve complex, real-world investment challenges. Solutions are used by portfolio managers and risk professionals across BlackRock — supporting investment decisions, improved portfolio positioning, winning client mandates. Works across all asset classes: equities, fixed income, derivatives, private markets.

## Role overview

Design, build, and deploy next-generation analytical tools that sit alongside and extend the Aladdin platform to enhance investment workflows: portfolio construction, investment research, relative value analysis, and increasingly **AI-enabled decision support**.

Work closely with investment teams to identify high-impact opportunities, rapidly prototype ideas, and iterate based on real-world feedback. Translate complex analytical capabilities into intuitive, fit-for-purpose solutions that integrate seamlessly into how investment teams operate.

Successful candidates wear multiple hats: hands-on analytics developers, internal technology advocates, and business-oriented project managers. Strong aptitude for learning the proprietary technology ecosystem; technical fluency in AI, Python, and database languages; experience building end-to-end analytical applications; ability to engage stakeholders; solid understanding of financial markets and instruments.
