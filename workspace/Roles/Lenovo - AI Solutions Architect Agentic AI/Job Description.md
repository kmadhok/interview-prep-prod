## About the role

Lenovo seeks an AI Solutions Architect for the Hybrid Cloud Solutions and AI Offering Engineering team within SSG. This senior-level role designs and delivers **agentic AI and generative AI solutions** across Lenovo's enterprise offering portfolio, producing customer-facing architectures and reusable solution IP consumed internally and deployed through Lenovo Professional Services and Managed Services teams globally.

The architect owns solution-level design across the full agentic AI stack: translating enterprise business problems into AI system architectures, designing multi-agent orchestration patterns, building RAG pipelines and LLM-powered workflows, and embedding AI governance (e.g., NemoClaw). Outputs span customer-deployable offerings and internally consumable reference designs that guide field delivery teams worldwide. NVIDIA AI Enterprise platform experience is valued as a key delivery layer, but the anchor is the ability to design AI solutions that solve real enterprise problems and translate into field-ready offerings. Remote-eligible with customer-facing responsibilities.

## In this role you will

- Design end-to-end **agentic AI and generative AI solution architectures** for enterprise customers, translating business/operational requirements into deployable AI system designs.
- Develop **multi-agent orchestration patterns and RAG pipeline architectures** using LangChain, LlamaIndex, CrewAI, AutoGen, and Semantic Kernel.
- Architect **AI governance and agent policy** solutions using NemoClaw and similar frameworks: inference governance, policy enforcement, responsible-AI guardrails.
- Produce **reusable solution IP**: reference architectures, solution blueprints, integration guides, and sizing frameworks consumable by PS and MS field teams globally.
- Design **LLM integration architectures**: model selection, fine-tuning strategy, prompt engineering patterns, context management, RAG for enterprise use cases.
- Develop **NVIDIA AI Enterprise** platform architectures (NIM microservices, NeMo Agent Toolkit orchestration, Triton inference optimization).
- Support **pre-sales engagements** as a senior AI architecture authority: customer-facing solution proposals, POC designs, architecture validation artifacts.
- Collaborate with Lenovo product teams and the NVIDIA Alliance to align architectures with hardware roadmaps and co-sell requirements.
- Maintain and evolve a library of reusable AI solution patterns distributed to WW GEO delivery teams.
