## About the Team

The Data Platform team sits within Scribd's Infrastructure organization and owns the data infrastructure, governance, and enablement that powers the entire company — from the Medallion Architecture and Unity Catalog in Databricks to the semantic and AI layers on top. High-impact team at the center of Scribd's AI adoption push: "we move fast, we set standards, and we care deeply about making data work for everyone — engineers, analysts, and business users alike."

## About the Role

Senior AI Data Engineer to lead the AI engineering workstreams on the Data Platform team. Three dimensions:
- **AI in the data layer** — building data infrastructure that enables AI use cases across the company.
- **AI in the data platform** — supporting platform stakeholders in building/shipping data products faster with AI.
- **AI in the development lifecycle** — helping the team accelerate development through AI tooling.

## What You'll Do

- Own the deployment path for Databricks Apps — infrastructure and guardrails that let non-technical users bring AI applications to production safely and consistently.
- Build the AI layer on top of Scribd's Medallion Architecture and Semantic Layer — connecting AI agents and agentic workflows to governed data, enabling non-technical users to get self-service answers.
- Build AI skills and agents on top of existing declarative tooling — interfaces for platform stakeholders to ship pipelines faster without deep expertise in every layer of the stack.
- Partner across the org to identify the right AI tools, frameworks, and agentic patterns that accelerate data product development and broader AI adoption.
- Identify and embed AI tools into how the Data Platform team writes, tests, and ships code.
- Establish guardrails to ensure AI-generated code, queries, and pipelines are correct, consistent, and production-ready.
- Help define/evolve data modeling and metadata patterns for AI use cases (context, documentation, discoverability).
- Mentor other engineers; define what great AI data engineering looks like at Scribd.
