## What's the opportunity?

As part of the GTM Analytics team, you will be the data partner for Fin's **Mindshare** function — the team responsible for building brand awareness, shaping market perception, and creating early engagement with target buyers. Focus is the **top of the funnel**: understanding how brand marketing, executive programs, experimentation, and upper-funnel initiatives drive awareness, reach, and engagement before prospects enter a sales cycle. Partner with Brand Marketing, Executive Programs, and GTM leadership to measure what's resonating, design experiments, and surface insights that inform how Fin shows up in the market.

## What will I be doing?

- **Top of Funnel Insights:** Own reporting and analysis for upper-funnel programs — reach, awareness, engagement, early-stage interest across brand marketing, content, executive outreach.
- **Brand Marketing Measurement:** Develop frameworks to assess brand campaign effectiveness — brand investment → audience reach, mindshare, downstream consideration.
- **Executive Programs Analytics:** Measure impact of executive dinners, outreach programs, high-touch engagement — which accounts are reached, how they engage, what signals emerge.
- **Experimentation:** Design and analyze experiments across Mindshare programs — test messaging, channels, formats, targeting.
- **Audience & Reach Analysis:** Segment by ICP, account tier, persona, geography.
- **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Partner with Brand Marketing, Executive Programs, Demand Gen, Sales — translate qualitative program goals into measurable outcomes.

## What skills do I need?

- **5–8 years** in analytics, marketing analytics, brand analytics, or related.
- Strong understanding of top-of-funnel and brand metrics — awareness, reach, engagement, consideration, audience composition.
- Experience designing and analyzing experiments (**A/B tests, holdout groups, incrementality measurement**).
- Strong SQL; data modeling tools (dbt) a plus.
- BI and visualization tools (Tableau, Looker, Streamlit).
- Comfort with qualitative and quantitative data — not everything at top of funnel has a clean conversion metric.
- Proven ability to partner with marketing and executive stakeholders, framing ambiguous questions into structured analyses.
- Clear communication — translate analytical findings into narratives that inform creative and strategic decisions.
