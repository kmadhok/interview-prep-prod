## Relevant project
The build closest to this role is a self-service analytics agent I designed and shipped over 67 BigQuery tables at Walmart. Business teams used to route every question through the one analyst who knew the schema; I built a sub-agent flow — Context Researcher, SQL Drafter, Validator, Devil's Advocate — that lets them ask directly and get back a validated answer, with 8 deterministic SQL rules and a 23-case golden-query suite as the eval gate. It's the only AI skill currently in active use by business teams on Data Ventures, and the reason it works is the same skill this job needs: translating a fuzzy business question into correct SQL against a messy multi-source data model, and being rigorous about data quality before an answer goes out. On the pipeline-and-scale side, I also automated ~20 recruitment workflows on a 68-table BigQuery platform using stratified-sampling pipelines that recruited 280,000+ panelists across 29 categories with zero duplicates, enforced at write time — a 3-hour manual task became ~3 minutes of setup. Between the two, I've done both the stakeholder-facing ad-hoc analysis and the data-quality-and-pipeline engineering this role spans.
