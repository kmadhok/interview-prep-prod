## About the Team

Blackstone Credit & Insurance AI ("BXCI AI") identifies and responds to AI-driven disruption risk across the BXCI portfolio. The team works directly with portfolio company leadership to assess competitive exposure, identify defensible assets, and reposition businesses. Engagements span business-model transitions, competitive repositioning, revenue-model changes, partnerships, acquisitions, and new channels designed to serve AI agents as customers.

## About the Role

Primary technical partner to the BXCI AI Operating team across the full engagement lifecycle — assessment → strategy → partner selection → hands-on execution. The role is a builder with strong business instincts, deeply fluent in the modern AI stack, able to form rapid, defensible opinions about unfamiliar technical systems.
