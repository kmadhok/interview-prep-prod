## About the Role

The Senior Production Engineering team sits at the heart of CoreWeave's reliability efforts. Partner closely with Support/CX teams to build, operate, and evolve internal tooling that enables a "Direct-to-Expert" support model at scale. Define and ship AI-assisted workflows, self-service diagnostics, and platform integrations that reduce time-to-resolution and improve customer experience across the cloud.

## What You'll Do

- Design, build, and own support-facing tools for case triage, intelligent routing, and expert engagement; integrate with incident and change management workflows.
- Develop AI-powered assistants and automations that accelerate root-cause discovery, knowledge retrieval, and resolution quality.
- Create/maintain dashboards, alerts, and signals that surface tooling issues early; integrate observability to reduce MTTR.
- Build self-service and guided diagnostics that empower Support/CX to resolve common issues and collect high-quality context for escalations.
- Codify reliability and support practices into services, APIs, and Kubernetes-native controllers/operators where appropriate.
- Partner with engineering leadership and stakeholders to prioritize roadmap, land adoption, measure business impact.
- Participate in an on-call rotation for the tooling you own.

## Minimum Qualifications

- 4+ years software or infrastructure engineering building and operating production services.
- Proficiency in **Go or Python** (or equivalent).
- Strong fundamentals in Linux, containers, and **Kubernetes**; comfortable debugging distributed systems.
- Observability (metrics/logs/traces) and using data to improve reliability/support outcomes.
- Incident management and steady-state operational excellence (progressive delivery, testing strategies, error budgets, fault-tolerant design).
- Comfort collaborating with Support/CX, Product, SRE, service owners.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Integrating/building support/ops tooling (ticketing/incident systems, status page, knowledge management, chat/alerting integrations).
- Automating manual workflows; stitching productivity platforms.
- **Familiarity with AI/ML tooling for retrieval, summarization, or copilot-style assistance.**
- Codifying operational practices into Kubernetes controllers, operators, or platform services.
