# Questions to Ask the Panel — Walmart Agent Builder

Panel: **Navin Kasa** (Agentic AI, Merch & Supply Chain — strategy/product), **Nathan Oswalt** (Agent Builder, supply-chain automation background), **Michael Ferrell** (Agent Developer, inventory/replenishment data background).

Rule of thumb: 2–3 questions per 1:1, weighted to that panelist's lane. Save the strongest for last — what they hear last is what walks into the debrief.

---

## For Navin (strategy, scope, conviction)

1. What does success for this team look like at end of 2026, and how would you know you hired the right person for this seat?
2. The JD is explicit that success is sales growth, cost savings, and time savings — not number of agents shipped. Which of those three is the team under the most pressure to deliver against right now?
3. When you picture the highest-leverage agent this team ships in the next year, what workflow does it touch — and what's blocking it from existing today? Technical, data access, or stakeholder buy-in?
4. What's the org bet behind a small central agent-builder team vs. embedding builders inside each business function? Where does that model strain?

## For Nathan (peer, supply-chain automation roots)

5. What's the actual cycle time from "I see a workflow worth automating" to "agent in production" — and where's the bottleneck?
6. You spent years on physical automation — depalletizing, WMS, simulation. What carries over to agent work, and what doesn't?
7. Tell me about a workflow you tried to agentify and walked away from. What killed it?
8. Where are you keeping humans in the loop today, and where are you betting you can take them out in six months?

## For Michael (peer, inventory/replenishment data roots)

9. Coming from the inventory data-solutions side, what surprised you most about how agent work is different?
10. How do you get data access when you need it for a new agent? Is that the limiting factor, or is it stakeholder buy-in?
11. What's the boring part of this job nobody warned you about?
12. If you could rewind to your first 30 days here, what would you do differently?

## Use with anyone (pick one per conversation, don't repeat across panelists)

13. What kind of person has succeeded on this team so far, and what kind hasn't?
14. What's a recent decision the team made that you'd reverse if you could?

## The closer — use with at least one panelist, ideally the last

15. Is there anything from our conversation today that gives you hesitation about my fit? I'd rather address it now than wonder later.

---

## Quick pick if you only have time for one question

- With Navin → #2 (which corner of the success triangle is under most pressure)
- With Nathan → #5 (cycle time + bottleneck)
- With Michael → #10 (data access vs. buy-in as the limiting factor)
- With anyone, as the closer → #15
