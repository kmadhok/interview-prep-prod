## About HappyRobot

HappyRobot is the infrastructure for enterprises to build and orchestrate AI workforces. Their AI workers don't just communicate — they make decisions, take action, and run operations autonomously across voice, email, and enterprise systems. Born in Y Combinator (S23) and backed by a16z and Base10 with over $60M raised, they power critical operations for global enterprises worldwide.

The platform is battle-tested in demanding environments where AI has real consequences. They started in logistics, built their own voice stack, models, and orchestration layer from the ground up, and are now bringing that infrastructure to every enterprise that runs the real economy.

HappyRobot helps enterprises like DHL & Naturgy put agents to work in complex environments — autonomous AI workers capable of communication and coordination, deployed at scale across core business functions (lead qualification, payment collection, service request, technician dispatch). The platform captures context and gets smarter from every agent interaction.

Company stats at filing: 161 total employees, ~1689% company-wide 2-year growth, ~300% engineering 2-year growth, median tenure 0.6 years.

A versatile engineer who thrives in a role that blends customer engineering with technical development. You will work closely with customers from onboarding through ongoing use of HappyRobot's AI platform, ensuring they maximize the value of the solutions.
