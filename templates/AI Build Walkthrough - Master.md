# AI Build Walkthrough — Master

## The 4-Beat Scaffold (memorize the beat names, not the words)

1. **Problem** — user, pain, stakes, and why the problem mattered.
2. **Build** — architecture, tools, judgment calls, and ownership.
3. **Eval** — success criteria, test design, failures, and iteration.
4. **Adoption + what's next** — outcome, users, operational reality, next step.

- Patterns to avoid in every walkthrough.

## Tells that signal builder-with-judgment (drop in naturally, don't force)

- Concrete decisions that show technical and product judgment.

## Drill pattern

- How to rehearse the four beats and likely follow-ups.
