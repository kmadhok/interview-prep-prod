# Tell Me About Yourself — Master

**Purpose.** One canonical TMAY spine plus archetype variants.

**The spine doesn't change.** Five beats, ~90 seconds spoken, ends on a clean
closing line that cues the follow-up you want.

---

## The spine (role-agnostic)

1. **Present.** Where you are now, in one line.
2. **The shift.** The inflection that changed your trajectory.
3. **What grew out of it.** The through-line of what you've built/done.
4. **Recognition.** Proof point — award, promotion, outcome.
5. **Why this role.** [Variant-specific closing line — see below.]

---

## How to use this file

- Practice the full version of the chosen variant out loud until the beat order is automatic.
- In the interview, glance only at the bolded hook line.
- The 5 section headers (Present → Shift → Built → Recognition → Why) are your recovery map.
- End cleanly, then stop. Silence cues the interviewer's follow-up.
