## Team context

The **Production Finance Operations & Innovation (O&I)** team supports the Production Finance team and cross-functional partners on data projects. O&I facilitates informed decision-making by providing analysis to stakeholders, including senior leadership and other departments. Mix of quick-turnaround ad-hoc reports and long-term analyses, strategic thinking, and stakeholder management.

The ideal candidate has strong data skills and a passion for innovation, with interest in Production Finance or the Entertainment industry. Highly collaborative, fast-paced setting.

---

## Responsibilities

- **Data Extraction & Transformation.** Extract, clean, and transform data from core systems including the Global Spend Report data model, Payroll Accounting Systems, and the Netflix Production Finance HUB. Aggregate and correlate data to report on job counts, vendor spend, and location spend across global markets.
- **Cross-Functional Collaboration.** Work closely with Production Finance, Public Relations & Government Relations, Finance & Strategy, Data Science & Engineering, and Studio Tech Solutions. Act as a proactive thought partner translating complex business needs into actionable data solutions.
- **Ad Hoc & Strategic Analysis.** Engage in rapid-turnaround ad-hoc (show-level spend, vendor breakdowns, cast/crew location analytics) AND longer-term strategic projects (trend analysis, workflow optimization).
- **Technical Development & Workflow Improvement.** Develop expertise in existing data models, identify data inconsistencies, recommend improvements to data collection and validation. Explore data tables owned by adjacent partners to enhance coverage and integration.
- **Data Visualization & Reporting.** Analyze structured datasets to develop metrics, dashboards, and trend reports using Google Sheets, Looker, Tableau, or similar. Present insights clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- **System & Tool Experimentation.** Proactively self-train and experiment with new tools/technologies to improve analytics workflows.
- **Production Finance Team Support.** Guide Production Finance executives on data access, analysis, and reporting. Respond to data pulls, insights, and custom analyses as needed.
- **Data Quality & Issue Resolution.** Monitor data integrity and troubleshoot urgent issues affecting coverage/quality.
- **Documentation & Knowledge Sharing.** Maintain clear documentation of data processes, workflows, and system configurations.
