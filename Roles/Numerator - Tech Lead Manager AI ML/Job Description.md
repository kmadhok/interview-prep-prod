## About Numerator

We're reinventing the market research industry. Numerator empowers the world's leading brands and retailers with insights into consumer behavior and the influencers that drive it. (Recently merged with Kantar Worldpanel; ~2,800 employees; caters to a large share of Fortune 100.)

## About the Role

Numerator is looking for a hands-on **Tech Lead Manager** to join its growing Machine Learning team. This is a **50/50 player-coach role**: you'll directly manage a small team of ML engineers while continuing to write code, design systems, and ship GenAI features yourself. You'll work with an established, rapidly evolving platform that handles millions of requests and massive data volumes, and you'll be responsible for both the team's technical direction and the growth of the people on it.

Historically the team focused on reducing COGS through ML automation. That work continues — and they're now also building **agentic experiences** for clients and internal stakeholders. You'll help shape both the technical roadmap and how the team operates as they expand into this space.

## How You'll Spend Your Time

- Manage and grow a small team of AI software engineers — 1:1s, career development, performance, hiring, day-to-day unblocking.
- Stay deeply technical: contribute to the codebase, design GenAI systems, own meaningful slices of delivery alongside your team.
- Apply agentic LLM patterns (tool use, multi-step reasoning, orchestration) to automate high-complexity tasks that previously required human judgment.
- Design and build GenAI-powered solutions for complex NLP tasks — NER, classification, information retrieval, summarization, and structured output generation.
- Partner closely with the team's PM and adjacent engineering teams to scope, prioritize, and ship.
- Translate ambiguous business problems into well-scoped technical work — and help engineers learn to do the same.
- Stay current with the fast-moving GenAI landscape and translate new capabilities into practical team impact.

## What You'll Bring

- **2+ years of engineering or data science management experience — 4+ direct reports, performance conversations, hiring.**
- 4+ years of hands-on ML or GenAI engineering experience, including production systems.
- Strong practical GenAI fundamentals: LLM APIs, context engineering, RAG, tool/function calling, agents, and evaluation methodology — you understand why these techniques work.
- Technical judgment: scope ambiguous problems, make sound build-vs-buy / custom-vs-off-the-shelf calls, balance shipping speed with maintainability.
- Data acumen: critically assess a dataset, spot distribution problems, reason about how data quality affects downstream model and business outcomes.
- Product orientation: engage with business context naturally, partner with PM as an equal, translate ambiguous requirements into well-scoped solutions.
- People-first management style: grow engineers through coaching and stretch work, give direct and timely feedback.
- Solid Python and software-engineering fundamentals: clean, testable code, REST API design, debugging, CI/CD.
- A genuine habit of self-improvement: follow the field, experiment with new models and tools.

## Nice To Haves
