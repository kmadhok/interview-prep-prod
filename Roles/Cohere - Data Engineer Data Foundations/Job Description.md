## Why This Role

Building the foundational infrastructure for how the world deploys AI. As a member of the Analytics & Data Insights team, tackle problems without textbook answers, launch new products, and help enterprises understand what foundational AI means for their bottom line.

## As a Data Engineer, You Will

- Work directly on new customer experiences built on one of the most advanced AI systems in the world.
- Collaborate daily with researchers and engineers.
- Run implementations end-to-end and see initiatives through to real outcomes.
- Partner across research, marketing, sales, and finance to help define how Cohere grows — recommendations feed directly into products and strategy.

## You May Be a Good Fit If You Have

- 5+ years working on production-grade data processing systems.
- Strong command of Python and SQL.
- Experience with distributed data processing frameworks (Apache Beam, Spark, or Flink) and large-scale data system design.
- Ability to transform unstructured data into performant datasets in relational databases and blob storage.
- Familiarity with modern analytics stack tooling (BigQuery, Airflow, dbt).
- Knowledge of Java or Golang (nice to have).
- Experience with Kubernetes (nice to have).
- Genuine excitement about AI — follow the research, have opinions, enjoy being in the weeds.
- Comfort operating at the edge of what's known; desire to build something genuinely new.
