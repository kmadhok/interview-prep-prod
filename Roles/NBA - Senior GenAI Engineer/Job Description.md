## Position Summary

The NBA's Enterprise Generative AI team is seeking a highly skilled and visionary Software Engineer to lead the development, deployment, and scaling of cutting-edge Generative AI applications that reimagine how internal IT systems and business operations function. Build foundational AI infrastructure and products that directly impact how the NBA operates across departments — from IT automation and support to business intelligence and productivity enhancement.

Collaborate across IT, business, and product teams to identify transformative use cases and implement scalable AI-first workflows.
