## About the Role

CenterGate Capital is seeking an individual to join the Value Creation team at the intersection of private equity operations and applied AI. You will identify, design, build, and deploy AI-powered solutions and agentic workflows across both the firm's internal operations and its portfolio companies. Working alongside the Data Engineer, you will translate strategic priorities into production-grade systems that reduce costs, accelerate revenue, and build competitive advantages. High-visibility role with regular interaction with portfolio company leadership and CenterGate's investment team.

## Key Responsibilities

- Conduct AI readiness assessments across portfolio companies; identify and prioritize high-ROI opportunities in back-office ops (finance, HR, procurement) and go-to-market (sales, marketing, pricing).
- Design and deploy end-to-end AI workflows from proof-of-concept through production, delivering measurable operating improvements.
- Build a reusable AI playbook of use cases, templates, and implementation guides deployable across new acquisitions.
- Automate internal firm workflows: deal sourcing intelligence, due diligence, portfolio monitoring, LP reporting, and knowledge management.
- Architect data pipelines and integrations with the Data Engineer to support AI workloads at scale.
- Present findings and recommendations to senior leadership, the investment committee, and portfolio company boards.
