## Relevant project
For my UChicago capstone, FTI Consulting brought us a real problem — their proposal team spent significant time assembling new proposals from past materials — and we built a RAG-to-proposal generator over FTI's own proposal library, deployed on AWS EC2, that cut proposal time about 30% and won Best in Show, judged by FTI partners. I built it section-aware — exec summary, bios, methodology, and pricing each got a tailored retrieval and generation step because their tone and structure differ — and deployed it as a real endpoint rather than a notebook because the grading rubric included whether a partner could actually use it. It's the closest thing I have to forward-deployed consulting work: a fixed client timeline, a deployed artifact at the end, and a partner audience.

On the data-architecture side, I replaced ~20 recruitment workflows we ran by hand with end-to-end automation on a 68-table BigQuery platform — a 3-hour task now takes ~3 minutes of setup, 280,000+ panelists recruited across 29 categories with zero duplicates. Deduplication is structural (a write-time check against a persistent allocations table, not a downstream cleanup), and every run emits a coverage report — the kind of large-scale, reliable, secure data solution design this role delivers for customers.
