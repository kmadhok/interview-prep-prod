## About Chainguard

Chainguard is the trusted source for open source. By delivering hardened, secure, and production-ready builds of all the open source software engineers and AI agents rely on, Chainguard helps organizations build faster, stay compliant, and eliminate risk. Customers include Anduril, Canva, Fortinet, HPE, OpenAI, Snap, Snowflake. ## The Role, In a Nutshell

Chainguard is becoming an AI company, and is hiring an AI Solutions Engineer to make that real in the daily work of every team. Dedicated, hands-on role with a clear mission: find the highest-leverage opportunities where AI can materially change the economics of how Chainguard operates — revenue, efficiency, and quality — and build the working prototypes that prove they work.

Part internal consultant, part builder. Partner with functional owners across the company (Engineering, GTM, Product, Operations), the AI Enablement Council, and AI Ambassadors to take prioritized use cases from idea to validated prototype. Turn promising ideas into internal workflow automations, reusable prompt systems, AI-enabled tools and assistants, and early integrations — using approved tools, keeping humans in the loop, holding a high security and quality bar.

Applied tooling and building role, not a research role. Work with tools like **Claude Code, the Anthropic API, Glean**, and the systems Chainguard already runs on. Reduce the frustration of great ideas stalling for lack of capacity or technical skill — and build the evidence base that tells the company where to invest next.

## What You'll Do

- **Find high-leverage opportunities:** Run listening tours and current-state assessments across functions. Identify and prioritize workflows where AI can deliver outsized impact, weighing impact against feasibility; focus on opportunities that materially bend the revenue/opex curve.
- **Prototype and validate:** Take prioritized use cases from idea to working prototype — internal workflow automations, reusable prompt systems and templates, AI-enabled tools and assistants, early workflow integrations.
- **Partner across the business:** Work with functional owners, the AI Enablement Council, and AI Ambassadors to understand workflow needs, validate opportunities, build the business case and evidence base.
- **Build responsibly:** Approved tools, Legal and Security review pathways, validated outputs, clear human review, strong data hygiene. No AI tool touches Chainguard/customer/personal data until cleared.
- **Make it visible and reusable:** Document what you built and learned. Share via Builder Hours, demos, showcases; surface recurring technical needs to the AI Enablement Council.
- **Set the standard for internal AI building:** Model responsible, high-quality AI building so prototypes can be handed off, scaled, and maintained by functional experts.

## What We're Looking For

- **Experience:** Minimum 4 years building software, automation, or internal tooling; excellent problem-solving; drives ambiguous zero-to-one work independently.
- **Applied AI tooling:** Hands-on with AI tools (Claude Code, Copilot, Cursor) in real work, and building on LLM APIs — tool/function calling, agents, prompt and context engineering, RAG, patterns like MCP.
- **Builder and integrator:** Wire together APIs, automations, integrations across SaaS systems (Slack, Google Workspace, GitHub, Linear) into reliable internal tools and prototypes.
- **Product and consulting sense:** Comfortable with non-technical stakeholders, framing problems, sizing impact, prioritizing by impact/feasibility — fuzzy needs into shippable prototypes.
- **Development:** Programming proficiency in at least one language, strong preference for **Go and/or Python**.
- **Responsible-use mindset:** Security, legal, data-handling guardrails.
- **Communication:** Strong communication; autonomous in a remote, globally distributed team.

Scope notes: not training/fine-tuning foundation models; does not own product roadmap or tool purchasing; never bypasses Legal/Security review.
