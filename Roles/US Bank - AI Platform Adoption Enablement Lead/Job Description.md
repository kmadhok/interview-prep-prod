## Role Overview

A **senior individual contributor** responsible for accelerating enterprise adoption of AI by guiding how AI applications are designed, built, deployed, and monitored. Deep technical expertise across the AI lifecycle with a strong focus on **application development patterns, observability, and operational excellence**. Partners across engineering, product, platform, and business teams to ensure AI use cases are implemented using scalable, secure, enterprise-aligned approaches. Defines best practices, enables teams to build responsibly, and ensures AI systems are measurable, reliable, and continuously improving in production.

Emphasis on: AI application design/development patterns · observability, monitoring, production performance · practical adoption of AI platforms/tools/services · scalable implementation of enterprise AI use cases.

## In this role you will

- Provide hands-on guidance to design/build/deploy AI applications across use cases (ML, GenAI, agentic AI)
- Define and operationalize **observability and monitoring frameworks** for AI systems (performance, drift, reliability, usage tracking)
- Guide teams to implement production-ready AI (scalability, resiliency, compliance)
- Partner with product/engineering to shape and refine AI use cases (feasibility, value, complexity)
- Drive adoption of enterprise AI platforms/tools/services via practical enablement and technical advisory
- Establish and document best practices, patterns, reusable approaches for AI app dev/deployment
- Support model/architecture/tool selection by use case
- Ensure AI implementations align with enterprise security, risk, governance

**Key areas:** AI app development & enablement (prompt design, orchestration, model integration, API deployment; architectural patterns for predictive ML / GenAI / agent-based; business→AI translation) · AI production deployment (MLOps: CI/CD, model registry, automated rollback; regulatory/security/privacy; SLAs/KPIs/observability) · AI architecture (scalable modular ecosystem: data ingestion, feature stores, training infra, inference; model governance — versioning, lineage, explainability, auditability; emerging-tech adoption roadmaps) · AI platform development (on-prem stack e.g. Airflow, Elasticsearch; self-service platforms/APIs/tooling) · observability & monitoring (metrics for performance/latency/cost/reliability/drift/UX quality; monitoring tools + feedback loops) · use-case guidance (trusted advisor, feasibility, risks/trade-offs/dependencies).
