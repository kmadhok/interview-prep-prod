## AI at Brex

AI Engineering at Brex builds intelligent, autonomous systems into the Brex platform. Teams develop AI agents that don't just surface insights but take action — optimizing spend, managing workflows, making real-time decisions on behalf of customers. By integrating proprietary financial data with product and platform infrastructure, they turn complex financial operations into simple, automated experiences.

## What you'll do

You'll be a **product engineer building Brex's Audit Agent** — an agentic system that reviews customer spend at scale and replaces manual work traditionally done by BPO teams. The agent reasons; the surrounding **product harness** is what makes that reasoning useful, trustworthy, and operable for real customers. That product harness is where you'll live: design and ship the workflows, surfaces, and data flows that turn the agent's output into something a reviewer can trust and act on — from the reviewer experience, to the contracts with Brex's financial system of record, to the feedback loops that make the agent better over time.

You'll move fluidly between backend and frontend. The role sits between deep agent design and the customer-facing UI. The majority of the work is backend (system design, data modeling, API shape); a meaningful minority is on the UI itself, building alongside frontend engineers.

## Responsibilities

- Build and ship customer-facing features in production — from sketch to rollout to iteration based on real customer feedback.
- Define the data contracts between the agent and the rest of Brex's financial system of record, and evolve them as the product sharpens.
- Stand up feedback and evaluation loops that gather product signals and close them with product fixes.
- Run experiments on customer-facing behavior (UI flows, prompt-driven product changes, new review modes) and make calls based on the data.
- Talk to customers and reviewers directly, bring what you learn back into the product, and prioritize what to build next.

## Requirements

- Strong track record shipping customer-facing features end-to-end across backend and frontend.
- Product mindset: reason about users, workflows, and outcomes first; comfort with ambiguity; willingness to talk to customers directly.
- Strong bias toward action — shipped things that didn't exist before in environments where the next thing wasn't handed to you in a ticket.
- Comfort working across team boundaries and pushing back when the seam isn't right (agent design, UX, data model all fair game).
- Strong backend foundation (system design, data modeling, API shape) and disposition to ship the UI yourself when the user problem needs it.
