## About the role

Figma is building a Sales AI Transformation function to embed AI across the sales workflow — reducing manual work, improving decision-making, simplifying how reps and managers operate day-to-day. As the founding Sales AI Engineer, you'll be a hands-on builder who designs and ships AI-enabled workflows that connect systems like Salesforce, Slack, and our data stack. You'll move quickly from prototype to production, measure adoption and impact, and create reusable patterns that help the broader GTM org build and scale what works. Full time; held from a US hub or remotely in the US.

## What you'll do

- Build and operationalize AI-powered workflows that improve daily execution for sales reps and managers (account insights, CRM hygiene, coaching workflows, outbound research/personalization).
- Integrate across the GTM stack using APIs, automation platforms, lightweight internal tools; ensure clean data flow and reliable execution.
- Apply LLMs pragmatically: choose the lightest safe solution, constrain outputs, build quality checks and monitoring so workflows can be trusted in production.
- Partner with SalesOps, Enablement, GTM Systems on requirements, rollout, governance, production deployment.
- Create reusable patterns (templates, agents, connectors, documentation) enabling distributed adoption rather than central dependency.
- Define success metrics for each workflow, track adoption and business impact, iterate based on real usage.

## We'd love to hear from you if you have

- Experience shipping production-grade automations or internal tools across GTM systems (Salesforce + adjacent tooling), including integration troubleshooting and data hygiene.
- Hands-on experience building LLM-powered workflows (OpenAI, Anthropic, or similar), with ability to explain how you ensured quality and safety.
- Strong coding ability plus comfort with SQL and operational data.
- Strong product and stakeholder instincts: translate ambiguous needs into shippable solutions, care deeply whether what you build is adopted.

## Added plus (not required)

- Familiarity with agentic patterns and experience instrumenting/monitoring AI workflows in production.
- Experience building internal Slack tooling or CRM-embedded experiences for sales teams.
- Salesforce development depth or strong equivalent CRM engineering experience.
