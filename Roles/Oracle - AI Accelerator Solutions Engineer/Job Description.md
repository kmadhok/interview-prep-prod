## Role Overview

OCI is looking for an AI Solutions Engineer that helps the **Business Operations team** transform manual processes into trusted, repeatable AI-assisted solutions. This role combines **workflow architecture, AI tooling, systems integration, and operational problem-solving** to deliver scalable solutions that drive efficiency and adoption.

## Responsibilities

- Partner with stakeholders to understand business processes, pain points, and desired outcomes
- Design and build AI-assisted workflows using modern AI development tools and automation frameworks
- Translate business requirements into reusable workflows, prompts, agents, skills, and integrations
- Connect files, APIs, databases, and enterprise systems to support workflow automation
- Develop validation and human-in-the-loop controls to ensure output accuracy and trust
- Troubleshoot workflow, data, integration, and AI-related issues
- Document solutions, operating procedures, assumptions, and support requirements
- Support stakeholder adoption through training, guidance, and reusable best practices
- Identify opportunities to scale successful solutions across teams

- Strong systems thinking, debugging, and problem-solving skills
