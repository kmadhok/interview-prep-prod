## About the role

Join the **Strategic Projects team**, reporting to the team's EM and ultimately into the co-founder/Chief Strategy Officer. The team exists to show developers and organizations why Tailscale should be their default connectivity substrate, both for internal infrastructure and embedded into customer-facing products. **Aperture, Tailscale's identity-aware LLM gateway, is one such initiative.**

This is an **AI-native team**. They use coding agents heavily as a force multiplier and expect every engineer to be a power user. Customers demand a high bar on code quality, security, and operational rigor, and the team treats that as a reason to use every tool available (including LLMs and coding agents) to ship fast without compromising it.

The team moves fast from idea to prototype and is growing the side energized by the path from prototype to dependable product. You'll work shoulder-to-shoulder with product-minded engineers from the moment an idea takes shape, co-owning the work of making it production-grade: stable, observable, maintainable, and ready for real customer load. You'll work directly with reference customers on integrations and own the production bar on prototypes that graduate into Tailscale's product surface area, ensuring the security, review, and operational practices around **agent-generated code** actually hold in production.

Fully remote; significant ambiguity; fast shipping cadence; async collaboration with highly technical customers.

## Key responsibilities

- Partner with product-minded engineers from the start, shaping prototypes (MCP gateways, LLM proxies, sandboxes, remote servers, similar emerging workloads) so the path to production is built in.
- Co-own the journey from "works in a demo" to "stable in production": hardening, error handling, observability, operational tooling.
- Own the production bar on prototypes that graduate, including the security and review practices that wrap around agent-generated code.
- Work with reference customers to integrate Tailscale (internal VPN-replacement / identity-aware networking and embedding into their products), with attention to reliability, maintainability, supportability.
- Find and close the long-tail gaps: edge cases, regressions, flaky behavior, missing tests, undocumented operational knowledge.
- Establish and maintain the review, testing, and security practices that let the team ship agent-generated code fast without compromising the bar.
