## Who Cohere is

Cohere trains and deploys frontier models for developers and enterprises building AI systems — content generation, semantic search, RAG, and agents. The team obsesses over building, moves fast for customers, and treats diverse perspectives as a requirement for great products. Mission: scale intelligence to serve humanity.

## Why this role

The role is the bridge between Cohere's **North platform** and enterprise engineering teams — guiding a group of engineers to deliver scalable agentic AI solutions to clients in finance, healthcare, and telecommunications. Customers include RBC, Dell, and LG CNS.

## In this role you will

- **Prompt Optimization** — Review, refine, and iterate on prompts for Cohere's Command model to improve performance across diverse customer agents and use cases.
- **Tool Development & Evaluation** — Collaborate with clients to refine custom tools and run rigorous evaluations to ensure alignment with their objectives.
- **Core Agent Development** — Contribute to defining and shaping core agents for out-of-the-box (OOTB) deployment on Cohere's North platform.
- **Customer-Centric Solutions** — Deeply understand customer business needs, then design and build tailored agents that deliver measurable improvements.
- **Metric-Driven Collaboration** — Partner directly with customers to define and track key metrics — quality, efficiency, accuracy — and ensure agents meet and exceed those goals.
