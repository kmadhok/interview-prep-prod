## The Role

Whatnot is hiring a Senior AI Engineer to build the internal tools, prototypes, and business workflows that put AI into the hands of every team at Whatnot, and to set the patterns the rest of the company builds on. Own ambiguous, cross-org bets end-to-end: find real problems, ship working software fast, harden what works, and scale how Whatnot gets value out of AI.

A high-velocity, high-judgment builder role. You can sit with a CX lead or ops manager, understand their workflow in an afternoon, and have a working prototype in their hands by the end of the week. You also operate above any single project: decide where the leverage is, define the reusable patterns and infrastructure others adopt, and raise the bar for how the whole org builds with AI. Fluent across the stack (frontend, backend, data, AI layer) and in the current generation of AI tools and production patterns: prompt engineering, RAG, MCP, agents, and evals.

**This is not a model-training or research role.** You'll build the tools, workflows, and integrations that make off-the-shelf AI dependable and useful inside Whatnot's systems and processes.

## What You'll Do

- Own ambiguous, cross-org AI bets end-to-end — identify the highest-leverage problems, decide what to build, drive from prototype to durable production tool.
- Build and ship a high volume of internal apps, prototypes, and automations — vague problem to working tool in days, then iterate to production quality.
- Define the reusable patterns and shared infrastructure the org builds on — reference architectures, internal libraries, MCP servers, eval harnesses, and templates that let others move faster and safer.
- Embed directly with teams across CX, Trust & Safety, ops, GTM, and EPD to find high-leverage problems and build alongside them.
- Wire AI into real business context — RAG and retrieval pipelines, MCP servers, and agentic workflows grounded in Whatnot's data, with appropriate PII and access controls.
- Integrate AI tools with internal systems and data sources via APIs, connectors, and event-driven workflows so automations act on real state.
- Scale the leverage — package successful builds into reusable skills and playbooks; level up the company through enablement sessions, boot camps, and mentorship.
- Stay ahead of the AI landscape; make build-vs-buy calls.

## Who You Are

- A prolific builder with senior judgment: 4+ years industry experience at scale; ship fast, iterate with real users, measure yourself by problems solved, tools adopted, leverage created.
- Deep applied AI fluency: 1+ years at scale building with current-gen LLM products (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) and production patterns: prompt engineering, RAG, MCP, agents, evals.
- Systems-integration chops — connected AI to real data/tools via APIs, webhooks, connectors; reason about reliability, latency, cost, access controls.
- A pattern-setter and force multiplier — defined how teams build, mentored engineers, raised the bar for an org.
- A strong communicator — sit with non-technical users, translate problems into software, teach them to build for themselves.
- Low ego, high agency.
