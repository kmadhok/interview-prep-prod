## The Role

Gong is seeking a Senior AI Transformation and Operations Engineer who can operate at the intersection of business transformation and technical execution. Sitting within our IT organization, this role is the heartbeat of our internal digital transformation, defining the AI strategy that creates sustainable competitive advantage while ensuring our enterprise AI operations are governed, consistent, and scalable.

You will partner cross-functionally across Talent, Sales, Support, and Go-To-Market to identify the highest-value AI opportunities, then drive them from concept to measurable business outcome. At the innovation level, you will shape high-level AI objectives and build proof-of-concepts that demonstrate real ROI. At the operations level, you will ensure our enterprise AI spend delivers maximum value.

This is an individual contributor role designed for a "scrappy builder" who thinks like a business strategist and executes like an engineer.

## Responsibilities

### Innovation

- **AI Use Case Identification** — identify internal AI use cases that drive the most value.
- **Proof of Efficacy** — build and deploy POCs independently to demonstrate ROI before scaling.
- **Business Partnership** — coordinate AI project execution across Talent, Support, Sales, Go-To-Market.

### Operations (the "Guardrails")

- **AI Stack Operations** — ensure the enterprise AI stack delivers maximum value: managing models, performance tuning, token management.
- **AI Process Standardization** — consistent AI processes: prompt libraries, system approvals.
- **Performance Monitoring** — build dashboards to track SLAs/SLOs (latency, accuracy, uptime) and monitor usage, cost, error rates.

**Scrappy Builder:** hands-on-keyboard; take an idea from a stakeholder and turn it into a working agentic workflow without external engineering resources.
